# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, and business trends using **SQL and Power BI**.

The project follows an end-to-end data analytics workflow, from data exploration and SQL analysis to interactive Power BI visualization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The goal of this project is to answer important business questions such as:

* What are the overall sales and profit trends?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which categories perform best?
* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which regions have the strongest sales performance?
* How do sales change over time?
* Which areas of the business may require attention?
